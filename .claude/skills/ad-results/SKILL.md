# Record results

The user brings the numbers: a paste, an export from Meta Ads Manager or
Business Suite, or screenshots. Never invent or estimate a number that wasn't
given; a missing figure is left as `—`.

## 1. Into LOG.md

Find each ad's row by its folder (the `utm_campaign`, or the ad's name in
Meta). Set status `live` with its start date, or `done` when it has stopped,
and fill **Results**:

- Paid: reach · link clicks · CTR · cost per click · spend, in pesos.
- Unpaid: reach · reactions · comments · shares · saves.
- Requests at `/start` from it, if the user knows them. These matter more
  than any click.

## 2. Read them honestly

- Compare only like with like: paid against paid, the same placement, the
  same audience, similar spend.
- A variant pair is the only thing that proves why one ad beat another. Two
  unrelated ads differing in five ways prove nothing about any one of them.
- Under about 1,000 reach, or a few days, say it is too early and record the
  numbers without a lesson.
- Comments are worth as much as numbers. A question people keep asking is
  the next ad, and maybe a missing FAQ on the front door — say so.

## 3. Into LEARNINGS.md

Add what was learned under its heading, saying which ads it came from and how
sure it is (one ad, a test, several). Strengthen an entry that this confirms
rather than adding a duplicate; delete one that this contradicts. When an
entry has held across three tests, move it into the rules in
`marketing/CLAUDE.md` and remove it here.

Tell the user in a few lines what worked, what didn't, and what the next ad
should try because of it.
