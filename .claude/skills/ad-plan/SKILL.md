# Plan ads

Read `marketing/CLAUDE.md`, `marketing/FACTS.md`, `marketing/LEARNINGS.md`
and all of `marketing/LOG.md` first. A plan that ignores what already ran,
or what LEARNINGS says flopped, is a plan to repeat it.

## What a plan is made of

Ask only for what isn't obvious: how long the run is, how many posts a week,
and whether there is money for paid ads this time. Then lay it out as a
table, one row per post: date, idea, app, for whom, layout, placements, paid
or unpaid, language.

Balance it rather than walking the app list in order:

- **Mostly one benefit each.** The ads that do the selling.
- **Some showing.** A real screen of the showcase church, through `screen.html`.
- **Some for the page, not for sale.** A useful thing for a church's week —
  a tip for Sunday, a question to answer in the comments — so the page is
  worth following and not only an ad feed. Never a Bible verse card with a
  logo on it; that is every church page's feed, not Ekkly's.
- **One test at a time.** Two variants differing in one thing (the hook, the
  picture, the language), run side by side, when something needs learning.
  LEARNINGS.md is filled by these.

Lean on what the week brings: Sunday for Schedules and Attendance, month-end
for Finances, a board meeting week for Minutes and Tasks, Christmas and Holy
Week for Events. Avoid making a holy day itself the ad's subject.

Lead with what LEARNINGS.md says works. With nothing there yet, start with
the apps that solve a pain every small church has: Schedules & Presentation,
People, Attendance, Finances.

## Write it down

Add each planned post to `marketing/LOG.md` as a row with status `idea` and a
folder name dated for when it goes out. Show the user the table, and offer to
make the first few with `/ad`.
