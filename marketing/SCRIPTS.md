# Video scripts

Short videos for Reels, Stories and the page, one idea each. Every claim is
from [FACTS.md](FACTS.md); every rule in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) applies, the
Meta ones included (talk about churches, never about the viewer's faith).

Three kinds, marked on each script:

- **Drawn**: made from scene types in `templates/video.js` (`pile`, `mark`,
  `apps`, `link`, `lines`, `close`) and rendered to MP4 with
  `render.mjs video`. Copy `ads/2026-10-11-one-place/reel.html` and change the
  scenes. Ready to make today.
- **Screen**: a phone recording of the app. Waits for the showcase church
  (CLAUDE.md → The showcase church): never a real church's members on screen.
- **Camera**: someone filmed talking. Only the owner, or a real volunteer with
  their church's written OK noted in the brief. Never an actor playing a
  church member.

All of them read with the sound off: the words are on screen, and a voice, if
there is one, says the same thing. Music is added in Meta's editor from its
licensed library, not baked in.

The first 2 seconds are the hook. Every script ends on the ask:
`ekkly.online/start`, “ask for your church”, never “sign up free”.

When one is made, it gets a folder in `ads/` and a row in LOG.md, and its
heading here says where.
