# Bagyo sa Linggo

**App:** Events (`events`)
**For:** pastors and secretaries who have to call off a service in a storm.
**The one idea:** call off one date in a tap, and the right people hear.
**Language:** Taglish
**Placements:** Reels and Stories, FB + IG (`reel.html`, 1080×1920, voiced,
with sounds and the app’s own *Still* track under it); unpaid post only, when a storm is forecast; never a paid ad pinned to a disaster

**The voice is an English one reading Tagalog**, spelled out for it (`reel.html` has each line’s spelling beside what it means). A Filipino listener will understand it and will hear that it is not a Filipino speaker; have someone listen before it runs paid.
