# One place for the church's records

**App:** Ekkly as a whole (People, Schedules, Attendance, Song List, Minutes,
Finances, Prayer Concerns)
**For:** pastors and church secretaries of small Filipino churches whose
records live in group chats, notebooks and spreadsheets.
**The one idea:** the lists a church keeps twice, in one app, so its people
have more time for the ministry.
**Language:** English
**Placements:** Reels and Stories, FB + IG (`reel.html`, 1080×1920, 46s,
voiced, with sounds and the app's own *Morning* track under it; it still
reads with the sound off)
**Link:** `https://ekkly.online/start?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=2026-10-11-one-place`
(`utm_source=instagram` for the Instagram placement)

Made with `node marketing/scripts/render.mjs video marketing/ads/2026-10-11-one-place/reel.html`;
the cover is `out/reel.png` (the whole pile of chores, at 9.8s).

## The script

| Time | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0–11s | Four cards drop onto the table, a little askew, each as it is said: *Sunday’s roster, lost in the group chat* · *The roll, kept in three notebooks* · *The offering in a spreadsheet nobody can open* · *The minutes, typed up twice*. Then they fall away. | The same four lines. |
| 11–15s | Ekkly’s mark lights pane by pane, a chime for each. *Ekkly. Your church’s records, in one app.* | “Ekkly keeps your church’s records in one app.” |
| 15–28s | Seven apps, one at a time, each dropping into a row: People, *Everyone’s details, one tap away* · Schedules, *Sunday without the scramble* · Attendance, *Count a service in a few taps* · Song List, *Find a song by any line* · Minutes, *Minutes done before you get home* · Finances, *The treasurer’s report, done* · Prayer Concerns, *No prayer request forgotten*. Then: *All in one app.* | “Everyone’s details, and Sunday’s lineup. The head count, the songs, and the minutes. The treasurer’s report, and every prayer request. All in one app.” |
| 28–35s | *Its own link. Its own colours.* `yourchurch.ekkly.online` types out, its colour changing as the swatches change. *Opens in any phone’s browser. Nothing to install.* | “Every church gets its own link, and its own colours. It opens in any phone’s browser. There’s nothing to install.” |
| 35–39s | *Less time on the lists. More for the ministry.* | The same, “More *time* for the ministry.” |
| 39–46s | The wordmark. *Ask for your church.* *Free for a month. Only the apps you need.* `ekkly.online/start` | “Ask for your church, at ekkly dot online. Your first month is free.” |

The voice was checked by transcribing the finished MP4 back to text: every
line came back as written.
