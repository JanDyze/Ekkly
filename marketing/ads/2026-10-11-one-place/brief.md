# One place for the church's records

**App:** Ekkly as a whole (People, Schedules, Attendance, Song List, Minutes,
Finances, Prayer Concerns)
**For:** pastors and church secretaries of small Filipino churches whose
records live in group chats, notebooks and spreadsheets.
**The one idea:** the lists a church keeps twice, in one app, so its people
have more time for the ministry.
**Language:** English
**Placements:** Reels and Stories, FB + IG (`reel.html`, 1080×1920, 32s,
silent: it reads with the sound off, and music is added in Meta's editor
from its licensed library)
**Link:** `https://ekkly.online/start?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=2026-10-11-one-place`
(`utm_source=instagram` for the Instagram placement)

Made with `node marketing/scripts/render.mjs video marketing/ads/2026-10-11-one-place/reel.html`;
the cover is `out/reel.png` (the pile of chores at 4.1s).

| 28–32s | The wordmark. *Ask for your church.* *Free for a month. Only the apps you need.* `ekkly.online/start` |
